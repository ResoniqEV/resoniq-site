Single coaxial in the trunk, diffused through the rear shelf. Fills the cabin with engine character you feel in the seat. This is the core of the Resoniq experience.

2 · Phase 3

Under Rear Bumper

A weather-sealed external speaker mounted under the rear bumper simulates an exhaust note, audible outside the car and through open windows. Crackle sounds project outward for full effect.

3 · Phase 3 / 4

AVAS System Tap

The Audi e-tron ships with an AVAS (Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System) speaker in the front for pedestrian warning. Resoniq can tap this channel to add front-end presence to the soundstage, with no new holes drilled.

AVAS tap note: The e-tron's AVAS speaker is EU-mandated and active below ~20 km/h. Resoniq would take over above that speed, blending seamlessly from the pedestrian alert tone into the full engine profile. Zero new drilling, just a clean signal splice behind the front bumper liner.

Resoniq started as a hand-wired prototype in one car. This year it became a custom board designed for small-batch production. Here's how that's going.

Once v1 is fully proven, the fixes roll into a second revision, a small validation batch, and then the first production run.

What vehicles does Resoniq work with?
Any electric vehicle with an OBD2 port, which covers virtually every EV made after 2008. The current prototype runs on a real Audi e-tron and has been daily-driven and tested. Other EVs may require a short setup to map the correct OBD2 PIDs for throttle position and speed, but no hardware changes are needed.
Will this void my car's warranty?
No. Resoniq connects to your OBD2 port in read-only mode. It only reads throttle and speed data and never sends any commands to your vehicle. It's legally and technically identical to plugging in a scan tool or a diagnostic app. Nothing in your car's software or hardware is modified in any way.
How does the startup sound work?
When you switch on your car, the Resoniq unit detects power and automatically plays the startup sound for your selected profile: an engine crank that ramps up into the running idle loop. It's seamless and triggers without any button press.
How loud does it get? Can I control the volume?
Completely adjustable from the app. The speaker lives in the trunk and fills the cabin through the rear shelf. Most people run it between 40–70%, present enough to feel but not overwhelming. You can also tweak bass and treble independently, and mute it in one tap if you want silence.
What sound packs are available right now?
Two live packs today: the Audi R8 V10 (studio-recorded, 5-layer crossfade system) and Test Pack v1 (the original development pack). V8 American, V10 Italia, Flat-6, and F1 Hybrid are in production. You can try both live packs in the simulator on this page, no download needed. You can also suggest a sound pack, and the most-requested ones get built first.
Can I upload my own custom sound packs?
Yes, this is already live in the prototype. The web app includes an OTA (over-the-air) upload screen where you can push your own WAV files directly to the unit over WiFi. Record an idle, low, mid, high, and redline clip, upload them, and you're running a custom pack. Full format specs will ship with the production unit.
How do I install the hardware?
Under 10 minutes, no tools required. Plug the Bluetooth OBD2 dongle into your OBD2 port (under the steering column), mount the Resoniq unit in your trunk, connect your phone to the etron-sound WiFi network, and open the app. That's it. The production kit will include a fuse-tap cable for cleaner permanent power, so no dongles or cables hanging.
