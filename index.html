Speaker System

Layered audio. Real presence.

Phase 3 expands from a single interior speaker to a full directional sound stage — inside and out.

① Live Now

Interior Trunk Speaker

Single coaxial in the trunk, diffused through the rear shelf. Fills the cabin with engine character you feel in the seat. This is the core of the Resoniq experience.

② Phase 3

Under Rear Bumper

A weather-sealed external speaker mounted under the rear bumper simulates an exhaust note — audible outside the car and through open windows. Crackle sounds project outward for full effect.

③ Phase 3 / 4

AVAS System Tap

The Audi e-tron ships with an AVAS (Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System) speaker in the front for pedestrian warning. Resoniq can tap this channel to add front-end presence to the soundstage — no new holes drilled.

AVAS tap note: The e-tron's AVAS speaker is EU-mandated and active below ~20 km/h. Resoniq would take over above that speed, blending seamlessly from the pedestrian alert tone into the full engine profile. Zero new drilling — just a clean signal splice behind the front bumper liner.
